03 / CHAMPIONSHIP RACE PROGRESSION

One format.
Three events.

All three championship events follow the same fundamental competition architecture. Exact heat timings and bracket sizes will be issued by the race officials based on the final participant count.

01
STAGE 1

Qualification

Individual timed flight. The best valid qualification performance is recorded.

02
STAGE 2

Seeding

Qualification results determine the official pilot ranking and elimination seed.

03
STAGE 3

Elimination

Multi-pilot heats progress through Round of 32 → Round of 16 → Quarter-Final → Semi-Final, with stages scaled to the registered field.

04
STAGE 4

Championship Final

The final multi-pilot race determines the official 1st Place, 2nd Place and 3rd Place results.

EVENT DAYS20–21 November 2026 · Jain University Global Campus, Kanakapura · Detailed session schedule to be announced by the organisers.

06 / FAQ

Quick
answers.

Answers below are based on the supplied race-format and technical-regulations document.

What is the race progression?+

Qualification → Seeding → Elimination → Championship Final.

How is seeding decided?+

The best valid qualification performance determines the pilot's seed.

Can pilots use autonomous flight?+

No. Autonomous flight and autonomous racing systems are prohibited unless specifically authorised by the competition rules. The aircraft must remain under active pilot control.

What battery is allowed in WHOOP WARS?+

1S LiPo, with a maximum cell voltage of 4.35 V.

What battery is allowed in AERO RUSH and BLINDLINE?+

Maximum 6S LiPo.
