08 / FAQ

Quick
answers.

What are the three championship events?+

WHOOP WARS, AERO RUSH and FPV WARS: BLINDLINE.

What is the race progression?+

Qualification → Seeding → Elimination Rounds → Championship Final.

How is seeding decided?+

The best valid qualification performance determines the pilot's seed.

Can pilots use autonomous flight, GPS or AI racing?+

No. The regulations prohibit autonomous flight, autonomous navigation, waypoint navigation, GPS position hold, autonomous obstacle avoidance, automated racing and AI-based autonomous control. The aircraft must remain under direct pilot control.

What are the WHOOP WARS battery limits?+

WHOOP WARS uses a 1S LiPo configuration with a maximum cell voltage of 4.35 V.

What are the AERO RUSH and BLINDLINE battery limits?+

Maximum 6S LiPo.

Where can I get the complete technical rules?+

Use the official Technical Regulations PDF in the Rules section of this website.

How do I register?+

Registration is completed through the official Google Form. Use any REGISTER button on this website.
